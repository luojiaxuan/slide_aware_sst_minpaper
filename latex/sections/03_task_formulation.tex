\section{Task Formulation}
\label{sec:task}

At time step $t$, a streaming SST system has observed source speech chunks
$\audio_{\leq t}$ and previously emitted target tokens $y_{<t}$. It also
observes a live slide stream
$\slides=\{\langle s_k,a_k\rangle\}_{k=1}^{N}$, where $a_k$ is
the causal time at which slide $s_k$ becomes stable. The slide remains current
until $a_{k+1}$, with dwell time $D_k=a_{k+1}-a_k$. Future pages are unavailable
unless an experiment is explicitly labeled \emph{deck-known-in-advance}.

The system outputs a sequence of committed target tokens $y_t$ or a \textsc{wait} action.  Each committed token receives a timestamp so latency can be computed.  The objective is to maximize translation quality and context-sensitive accuracy while satisfying a latency budget.

\paragraph{Persistent evidence state.}
After each change event, a visual worker encodes $s_k$ exactly once and
atomically publishes an evidence state $E_k$. The state may contain OCR text,
terms, entities, formulae, layout and emphasis cues, chart relations, source
coordinates, and confidence. Its candidates have the schema
\begin{equation}
    e = (\mathrm{type}, \mathrm{content}, \mathrm{relation},
    \mathrm{region}, a_k, \mathrm{confidence}).
\end{equation}
The same $E_k$ is reused whenever $a_k\leq t<a_{k+1}$; a term
glossary is one projection of this state, not its complete representation. At
step $t$, a retriever returns candidates $\evidence_t$ conditioned on
$\audio_{\leq t}$, partial source hypothesis $\hat{x}_{\leq t}$, decoder
history, and evidence staleness.

\paragraph{Context policy.}
The policy maps each candidate to one of four decisions:
\begin{equation}
    \pi(e, t) \in \{\textsc{use}, \textsc{ignore}, \textsc{delay}, \textsc{downweight}\}.
\end{equation}
The selected evidence packet $\packet_t$ is deliberately small, containing at
most $K$ items. The audio policy never blocks for vision: if $E_k$ is not ready,
it emits from audio alone. This design prevents a whole slide from dominating
the prompt and makes evidence-ready misses and context overuse measurable.

\paragraph{Evaluation setting.}
We evaluate systems under fixed chunk sizes or wait-$k$-style policies and
report current, stale, timestamp-shuffled, and cross-talk slide conditions.
Latency accounting separates change detection, once-per-slide encoding,
dwell-normalized amortized cost, on-path retrieval, and end-to-end
computation-aware lagging.
