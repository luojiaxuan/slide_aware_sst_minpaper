\section{Limitations}

This study has several current limitations. First, machine-produced or partially
verified references cannot support fine-grained method ranking; headline claims
require independent or human references. Second, the 206-item speech-input
probe comes from one clean lecture and uses same-talk wrong slides, so it cannot
separate local evidence from domain priming or generic image presence. Third,
slide dwell time and slide-to-related-speech lead/lag have not yet been measured
on the complete long-form timeline; future pages must never leak into a live
causal condition. Fourth, structured VLM evidence may merely be better OCR;
beyond-OCR claims require preregistered layout, formula, chart, and emphasis
slices plus an oracle text-equivalent control. Finally, asynchronous processing
still consumes compute: change detection, cold encoding, readiness misses,
retrieval, and end-to-end computation-aware latency must all be reported.
